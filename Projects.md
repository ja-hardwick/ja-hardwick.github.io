### [PhD Project: The Fluvial Biogeomorphic Impacts and Feedbacks of Riparian Invasive Non-Native Plant Species (2022 - 2026)](/projects/phd-invasive-riparian/)
My thesis explores how invasive plants like *Impatiens glandulifera* influence riverbank erosion and sediment dynamics across spatial scales. Using field data and modelling reveal key biogeomorphic feedbacks and stress the need for improved riparian invasive plant species management.

### [Water of Dye Restoration (2025 - present)](/projects/water-of-dye/)
Working in the Water of Dye, an upland degraded catchment in NE Scotland, we seek to understand how different restoration interventions combine to influence geomorphology, hydrology and biodiversity responses at landscape scale.

### [Agricultral Expansion, Climate and Geomorphic change in the Kilombero Valley, Tanzania (2023 - present)](/projects/kilombero-valley/)
To improve our understanding how agricultural expansion and sociological factors drive geomorphic change in a tropical agricultural catchment. 

### [Roots and invasions: _Impatiens glandluifera_ (2023 - present)](/projects/roots-and-invasions/)
Project aims to understand the difference in rooting strategies and traits between invaded and un-invaded riparian communities.

### [CAESAR Lislfood SpatVeg: plant communities in landscape evolution modelling (2022 - present)](/projects/spatveg/)
To improve understand of interactions between vegetation and land surface process across spatial and temporal scales.

### [Equality, Diversity and inclusion in enviromental science (2023 - 2025)](/projects/edi-fieldwork/)
Project aims to understand how institutional policy protects/or doesn't environmental fieldworkers and how this intersects with protected characteristics. To better inform policy development to make fieldwork inclusive and safe for all.
